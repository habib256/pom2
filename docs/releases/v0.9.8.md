# POM2 v0.9.8

- **Sessions restore the whole machine:** profile, firmware, cards and
  media, //c included, and older sessions too.
- **Bank-aware debugger:** breakpoints and watches that know the memory
  bank, `.p2map` source breakpoints, and a built-in assembler.
- **3.5-inch writes:** WOZ and MOOF 3.5-inch disks can be written and saved.
  SmartPort FORMAT and EJECT now work.
- **Clearer disk handling:** a refused disk explains why. Insert disk and
  boot picks the right drive.
- **No more double-buffer flicker:** each band of the picture shows memory
  as the beam read it, like a real Apple II.
- **Configurable host joystick:** stick 1 is the Apple II joystick by
  default. Each paddle can be mapped to a stick, a trigger or any axis.
- **Accuracy:** NMOS undocumented opcodes, the //c Plus 4 MHz mode, and the
  //c and //c Plus mouse reaching every screen edge.
- **Sound, printers, network:** Echo+ TMS5220C voice, ImageWriter custom
  characters, and Uthernet, FujiNet and Super Serial fixes.
- **Many fixes** from bug hunts against MAME and AppleWin.
