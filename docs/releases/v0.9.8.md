# POM2 v0.9.8

- **Sessions rebuild the whole machine:** opening a session now restores its
  profile, firmware, CPU, memory and cards by itself, from older sessions too.
  Sessions now cover the //c family, writable and modified floppies, hard
  disks, host folders, program tapes and more cards. In the browser, automatic
  resume works on the //c. Session loads can no longer freeze the window.
- **A bank-aware debugger:** breakpoints, watches, symbols, profiling and
  history tell main, auxiliary, RamWorks, Language Card and //c firmware banks
  apart. A Source panel loads `.p2map` files for source-line breakpoints. A
  built-in assembler patches stopped RAM, and the Disk II inspector plots a
  full revolution of flux. Breakpoints are no longer missed or miscounted.
- **3.5-inch writes and SmartPort details:** WOZ and MOOF 3.5-inch disks can
  be written and saved with their original timing, and SuperDrive writes
  update MFM captures. SmartPort FORMAT and EJECT work, and a program that
  ejects a 3.5-inch disk really takes it out. Sweet-16 2IMG images now load.
- **Clearer disk handling:** a refused disk opens a dialog that says what the
  file is, which drive takes it and what to do. File > Insert disk and boot
  picks the right drive and boots, and the browser can import a disk from a
  local file. `--prodos-folder` works on the default setup, and host folders
  handle sparse files both ways.
- **CPU and machine accuracy:** the undocumented NMOS 6502 opcodes now compute
  real results and pass a 660,000-case CPU test set. The //c Plus runs its
  built-in 4 MHz accelerator, and holding Esc at reset or power-on drops it
  to 1 MHz, as on the real machine. The //c's 40/80-column switch is
  readable, and a //e Ctrl-Reset keeps a mixed screen.
- **Video and mouse:** VidHD shows the IIgs text and border colours used by
  ProDOS colour themes. The Le Chat Mauve RVB Graph is rebuilt from its own
  manual, with its colour registers and text colour. On the //c and //c Plus
  the Apple cursor now follows the pointer to every screen edge (A2DeskTop
  included). The AppleWin-style mouse honours GETCLAMP, TIMEDATA and
  mouse-off, and runs at 60 Hz until told otherwise, PAL included.
- **Host joystick you can configure:** by default the first analog stick is
  the Apple II joystick and the second drives PDL(2/3). In the Joystick panel
  each paddle can take either stick, the L2/R2 triggers or any raw axis, and
  Detect learns it from a move.
- **Printers and clock:** the ImageWriter II prints downloaded custom
  characters instead of garbage, and power-off clears half-received commands.
  A ThunderClock without its ROM dump now gives ProDOS the time.
- **Sound:** the Echo+ speaks through a real TMS5220C LPC voice and its two
  AY chips, and no longer stays late after disk turbo or fast speeds.
  Mockingboard, Phasor and Echo+ timers keep their settings across Ctrl-Reset.
  Sound devices keep up below 1× speed.
- **Network and serial:** the Uthernet II receives data and disconnects
  without being polled. Uploads are no longer cut short on disconnect, and a
  refused UDP packet keeps its socket. Uthernet I event handling and FujiNet
  extended calls are fixed. The Super Serial Card no longer loses bytes when a
  listing outruns a slow line.
- **Many accuracy and safety fixes:** bug hunts against MAME, AppleWin and
  datasheets fixed the ][/][+ keyboard switches, rewind length and timelines,
  old VidHD and //c Plus snapshots, and many media, input, serial and JSON
  edge cases. The AI control server no longer reaches arbitrary user files
  after a Finder or desktop launch.
