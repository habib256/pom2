# POM2 v0.9.5

- **Videx Videoterm** for the Apple ][ and ][+: the 80-column card in slot 3, running its real firmware over an HD6845 CRTC. Its ROMs now ship with POM2, so `PR#3` works out of the box.
- **Floppies save while they are still in the drive.** Every 5.25" and 3.5" image reaches its file about a second after the drive goes quiet — no eject needed.
- **Printing, detected the way programs detect it:** Apple's real Super Serial Card firmware with its DIP switches and cable lines, plus the original Grappler (1981) and the Apple Parallel Interface. The Abstraction Levels panel now flags a serial card running without its EPROM.
- **//c and //c+:** the external 3.5" drives enumerate correctly, the //c+ boots a mounted 3.5" disk, and the //c's built-in serial ports no longer read garbage when no card models them.
- **Sound:** a cleaner speaker; Mockingboard AY and 6522 fixes from an audit against MAME; Mockingboard and Phasor keep their pitch under a TransWarp or the //c+ at 4×; an optional Virtual ][ drive-sound bank for those who own it.
- **Two ways to lose 3.5" disk writes are closed** on the //c+ built-in drives: re-inserting the same disk under another path, and ejecting while the program was still writing.
- **The speaker no longer goes silent** for the rest of the session after a short host hiccup.
- **Pasting old Apple text works again:** a DOS 3.3 listing pasted from a file no longer loses the "T " of every PRINT.
- About forty further defects fixed across four bug hunts, each pinned by a test that fails without its fix.
