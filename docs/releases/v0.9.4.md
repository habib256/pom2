# POM2 v0.9.4

- An Apple //c or //c+ with no disk boots again: it prints "Check Disk Drive." instead of hanging on a blank screen.
- The //c's CPU-socket Mockingboard 4c has its own setting and stays asleep until a program calls it, so a //e's slot 3 card no longer follows you into a //c; Slot Configuration's connector lists now match the real machines.
- The TransWarp accelerator works on an Apple ][, ][+ or unenhanced //e: as on the real board, the card's own 65C02 runs the program while it holds the bus.
- `--blank-disk` creates a diskette that has never been formatted — unmagnetised, no address fields — so a program's INIT has real work to do.
- "Download missing from RetroBIOS" in the ROM Status panel can now complete the whole romset: 38 dumps instead of 18, each verified by size, CRC32 and SHA-256.
- Two more character sets in the picker: US Enhanced with the later MouseText (Return symbol and title bar), and ReActiveMicro's ReActiveText.
- Fifty defects fixed across three bug hunts, each with a test that fails without its fix: media write-back and write-protect, host folders, snapshot and rewind restores that lost state, printing, sound and the //c's Disk II after a SmartPort write.
- 3.5" drives turn at the right speed on the PAL profiles, where every revolution used to be 0.7 % too long.
