# POM2 v1.2.1

> [!WARNING]
> **First launch on macOS or Windows: you have to allow POM2 once.** The packages
> are not notarized by Apple or signed for Windows, so both systems block a
> downloaded copy.
>
> **🍏 macOS: "Apple could not verify POM2".** Drag **POM2** from the `.dmg` into
> *Applications* and try to open it. Click **Done**, then open the Apple menu →
> **System Settings** → **Privacy & Security**, scroll down to **Security**, click
> **Open Anyway** and confirm.
>
> A package older than v1.1.2 says *"POM2 is damaged"* instead and has no
> **Open Anyway**. Do not move it to the Trash. Run this in Terminal:
>
> ```bash
> xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/POM2.app
> ```
>
> **🪟 Windows: "Windows protected your PC".** Click **More info**, then
> **Run anyway**.

- **Speed x2 / x4:** the Disk II works again. At x4, DOS 3.3 read nothing and
  Dirtbike stayed on its boot screen; it now reaches its demo on the same cycle
  at x1, x2 and x4. The whole machine speeds up together: Mockingboard and
  Phasor timers, the mouse, E-Z Color, SNES MAX, Echo+, the Z80 cards, the
  Liron and the Workstation Card. Music keeps its pitch.
- **Video timing measured on a real PAL //e:** `$C019` and the //c's VBLINT
  are read one cycle earlier, mid-line switches are re-anchored, 80COL splits
  with PAGE2, and the floating bus matches the machine. arekkusu42's FLOATBUS
  and BEAMPOS checks pass on //e and //c, PAL and NTSC; DIX renders
  as before. Thanks to **Stéphane Champailler** (Accurapple) for the traces.
- **Disks:** mounting, ejecting or changing a bay's type while another disk
  arrives no longer flushes or ejects the newcomer. A damaged Disk II session
  is refused instead of crashing POM2, and a save diverted by an outside edit
  names the copy it wrote.
- **Network:** the built-in NAT can publish guest services to the LAN
  (`uthernet_nat_forwards`), and adds IPv6, fragments and more DNS record
  types. Six defects found by NeoST's audit are fixed, including a macOS
  memory leak per DNS lookup and a lookup that could freeze the emulator.
  Uthernet II is now tested against unchanged IP65 Telnet and Gopher clients.
- **Debugger:** while profiling, it shows how much of each frame went to
  interrupt handlers — a French Touch demo's Mockingboard player, for example.
- **E-Z Color, read off its 1982 schematic:** the card answers on all of
  `$C0n0-$C0nF` (A0 picks the port), its VDP keeps its own 59.92 Hz frame
  (from POM1), and its interrupt now goes to **NMI** as published, through
  jumper JP2 — IRQ (the old behaviour) and "no interrupt" remain one setting
  away. The slot bus gained the `/NMI` line to carry it.

Also: the ARM64 desktop AppImage now starts on Debian 12 / Ubuntu 22.04 and
newer (glibc 2.36, was 2.39), `--ezcolor[=N]`, `pom2_headless --pal --speed`, a lighter `DiskImage`,
and documentation checked line by line against the code (`POST /reset` takes
its kind in the JSON body).
