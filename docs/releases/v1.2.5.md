# POM2 v1.2.5

> [!WARNING]
> **First launch on macOS or Windows: you have to allow POM2 once.** The packages
> are not notarized by Apple or signed for Windows, so both systems block a
> downloaded copy.
>
> **🍏 macOS: "Apple could not verify POM2".** Drag **POM2** from the `.dmg` into
> *Applications* and try to open it. Click **Done**, then open the Apple menu →
> **System Settings** → **Privacy & Security**, scroll down to **Security**, click
> **Open Anyway** and confirm.
>
> A package older than v1.1.2 says *"POM2 is damaged"* instead and has no
> **Open Anyway**. Do not move it to the Trash. Run this in Terminal:
>
> ```bash
> xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/POM2.app
> ```
>
> **🪟 Windows: "Windows protected your PC".** Click **More info**, then
> **Run anyway**.

A reorganisation of the interface, the Debugger and the Screen Inspector,
for people who write Apple II software in POM2.

**Interface**

- **Three clean layouts** (View ▸ Layout): Emulation, Mini-Apple II (the
  screen above its keyboard) and Debug (Screen Inspector beside the
  Debugger). Each shows its own panels and closes the others.
- **Tidier menus.** Devices opens with Slot Configuration and Drives, then
  one submenu per kind of card. The debugging panels are in Tools. Every
  menu item has an icon.
- **F8 for full screen**, like F10 and Ctrl+Alt+F; Alt+Enter does it with
  the pointer captured.
- **Help**: Welcome and About are rewritten, Thank you rolls the names of
  everyone POM2 stands on, and Report a bug fills the issue with the state
  of the machine.

**Debugger**

- **A listing you can read**: fixed-pitch font, aligned columns, wheel and
  Page Up / Page Down, "Go to" an address or a label.
- **AppleWin's keys**: Space steps, Ctrl+Space steps over, Shift+Space
  steps out, Ctrl+Down runs to the cursor, Tab returns to the PC.
- **The machine's addresses are named** with no symbol file: `LDA $C055`
  reads "PAGE2 on", `JSR $FDED` reads "COUT". Your own symbols still load.
- **Where the machine is**, in five lines: CPU, video switches, memory
  banks, beam position (V and H, numbered as in AppleWin) and a cycle
  counter you can reset.
- **Slow motion** down to 0.1 %, and **Beam view**: the screen as the tube
  has it at this cycle.

**Screen Inspector** (Tools menu)

- The picture really displayed, with the beam drawn on it.
- The mode and page of every line, with a mark where a switch is thrown
  inside a line.
- Under the mouse: coordinates, mode, page, memory address and the value
  the beam read.
- Saves the picture as PNG and the video pages as the beam fetched them.

**Also**

- **Speaker SFX editor** (Tools menu), from POM1: design a sound for the
  1-bit speaker and export the assembler table.
- **One beep at start-up**: POM2 no longer boots the machine twice.
- **"Download missing ROMs" replaces a wrong dump** and keeps the old file.
