# POM2 v1.2.2

> [!WARNING]
> **First launch on macOS or Windows: you have to allow POM2 once.** The packages
> are not notarized by Apple or signed for Windows, so both systems block a
> downloaded copy.
>
> **🍏 macOS: "Apple could not verify POM2".** Drag **POM2** from the `.dmg` into
> *Applications* and try to open it. Click **Done**, then open the Apple menu →
> **System Settings** → **Privacy & Security**, scroll down to **Security**, click
> **Open Anyway** and confirm.
>
> A package older than v1.1.2 says *"POM2 is damaged"* instead and has no
> **Open Anyway**. Do not move it to the Trash. Run this in Terminal:
>
> ```bash
> xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/POM2.app
> ```
>
> **🪟 Windows: "Windows protected your PC".** Click **More info**, then
> **Run anyway**.

- **The ][+ firmware is fixed at the source.** The `apple2p.rom` POM2
  downloads had the Monitor's reset entry as its IRQ/BRK vector, so every
  interrupt on a ][+ dropped to the `*` prompt. POM2 has patched it in
  memory since v1.2.1; the file itself is now Apple's (`$FA40`, the page MAME
  and AppleWin carry). ROM Status flags an old copy and **Download missing
  ROMs** replaces it.
- **Music and sound out of the machine.** Audio Mixer ▸ Recording saves the
  Mockingboard's music as **VGM** (every AY write at its exact sample, both
  chips) or **YM**, and what you hear as **WAV**.
- **Debugger:** **Trace to file** writes every instruction (PC, physical
  bank, bytes, disassembly, registers, cycles) with a format of your choice;
  **Export callgrind** opens the profiler in KCachegrind, routines named from
  a cc65 `.dbg` or a symbol file. The Source and Symbols panels read cc65's
  `.dbg` directly, and Step Over now stops after a ProDOS MLI call.
- **The Passport MIDI Interface** (slot 2, for Ultima V and others): its
  music goes to the computer's MIDI synthesizer and can be recorded as a
  `.mid` timed by the emulated clock.
- **Disks:** a WOZ whose bit cell is not a whole cycle (Prisoner 2) boots;
  the ImageWriter's paper is one continuous fanfold; Finder and Windows can
  open disk images with POM2 without taking them over from other programs.
- **In the browser:** a link can fill several drives
  (`?s6d1=…&s6d2=…`, `?boot=s5d1`), **File ▸ Copy link to this setup**
  writes one for what is running, and `?embed=1` puts POM2 in another page
  with `postMessage` events (boot, pause, running, error).
- **For developers:** `pom2_headless` runs 6502 unit tests on a whole Apple
  II (`--test-port`, `--load`, exit status from the program), records VGM /
  YM / WAV and traces or profiles a run; `pom2_corpus --video` records every
  emulated frame; `pom2_prodos` edits `.2mg` images; `--mount sNdM:path`
  fills any drive from the command line; `--help` lists every environment
  variable.

Also: seven rounds of bug hunting since v1.2.1 — among them a paint editor
one alt-tab could break, a FASTChip set slow that ran fast, ROM files of any
size, hostile zips in the browser, the //e's `$C800` window under the internal
ROM, the /NMI line in snapshots, and two rounds over this release's own new
features before it shipped (`docs/audits/bug-hunt-2026-11-19.md`). deater's
six demos and Apple's Service Disk joined the compatibility corpus, and the
`$C019` VBL edge is checked on every push.
