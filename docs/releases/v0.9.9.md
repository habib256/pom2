# POM2 v0.9.9

- **Browser:** `?url=` boots a disk image from another site, the screen
  opens on its own, and the audio no longer crackles.
- **Input:** the host mouse can drive paddles 0 and 1, and the //e
  Shift-key mod is an option.
- **Disks:** choose a disk's sector order by hand, format a WOZ on any
  track, and a macOS command-line disk boots once.
- **//c and //c Plus:** Ctrl-Reset keeps the 3.5-inch drives, and SmartPort
  calls answer like the Liron firmware.
- **Accuracy:** WDC `WAI`, the ThunderClock interrupt from power-up, and a
  cassette recording that follows the rewind.
- **Proven on real software:** DIX, Arlequin and Extasie on the Féline, and
  A2osX printing and networking through POM2's cards.
- **`pom2_core` SDK:** the //c and //c Plus, the mouse, a bus watch and the
  display state.
- **Many fixes** from bug hunts against MAME and AppleWin.
