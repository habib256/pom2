# POM2 v1.1.0

**New hardware.** Eight cards — Microsoft SoftCard II, PCPI Applicard,
Premium SoftCard //e, ALS CP/M Card, Saturn 128K, Videx UltraTerm, DAN ][
Controller, E-Z Color (TMS9918A, with its own Card Monitor tab) — and an
OS-call trace (ProDOS MLI, SmartPort, Pascal BIOS, Monitor, CP/M BDOS, …).

**No copyrighted firmware ships.** A Disk II boots on POM2's own boot PROM
and data separator; a first launch offers to download the rest.

**Closer to the silicon.** Every one of the 801 "MAME/AppleWin says" comments
was audited against the upstream history *and* the primary documents
(33 reports in `docs/audits/`): 3 major and 26 minor divergences fixed — a
`SAVE` that disarmed the //c motor timer, a 6551 IRQ the datasheet forbids,
a Z8530 write that stopped the transmitter, a FLUX track losing one
transition per revolution, a same-value 6522 store that re-strobed the AY,
five ImageWriter power-on defaults — and ~35 places where MAME or AppleWin is
wrong and POM2 now says so. The rule that came out of it: every hardware claim
in the code carries its **evidence grade**, `[A]` measured on real hardware
down to `[F]` admitted unknown, and a citation of another emulator names its
revision.

**Also:** disks from a URL / `.zip` / `.gz`; one `NAME#TTAAAA` host-file
convention for the ProDOS folder, `pom2_dos33` and `pom2_cpm`; a `Measured`
display aspect; an audio buffer that survives a Raspberry Pi and a phone
browser; the floating bus matching Sather's scanning maps address for address.
