# POM2 v1.1.0

**New hardware.** Eight cards — Microsoft SoftCard II, PCPI Applicard,
Premium SoftCard //e, ALS CP/M Card, Saturn 128K, Videx UltraTerm, DAN ][
Controller, E-Z Color (TMS9918A, with its own Card Monitor tab) — and an
OS-call trace (ProDOS MLI, SmartPort, Pascal BIOS, Monitor, CP/M BDOS, …).

**No copyrighted firmware ships.** A Disk II boots on POM2's own boot PROM
and data separator; a first launch offers to download the rest.

**Closer to the silicon.** All 801 comments that cite MAME or AppleWin were
re-read against the primary sources — datasheets, Sather, Apple's technical
notes, format specifications, ROM disassemblies — in 33 reports under
`docs/audits/`. Where POM2 and those sources disagreed, 3 major and 26 minor
fixes followed: a `SAVE` that disarmed the //c motor timer, a 6551 IRQ the
datasheet does not allow, a Z8530 write that stopped the transmitter, a FLUX
track losing one transition per revolution, a same-value 6522 store that
re-strobed the AY, five ImageWriter power-on defaults. Where POM2's reading
of a datasheet differs from another emulator's, the divergence is now
documented in the code rather than silently aligned. Every hardware claim
carries its **evidence grade**: **A** measured on real hardware, **B** primary
document, **C** ROM disassembly, **D** another emulator (with its revision),
**E** unsourced, **F** admitted unknown — and a lower grade never overrides a
higher one.

**Also:** disks from a URL / `.zip` / `.gz`; one `NAME#TTAAAA` host-file
convention for the ProDOS folder, `pom2_dos33` and `pom2_cpm`; a `Measured`
display aspect; an audio buffer that survives a Raspberry Pi and a phone
browser; the floating bus matching Sather's scanning maps address for address.
