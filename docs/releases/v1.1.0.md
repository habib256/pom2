# POM2 v1.1.0

Fifty changelog entries since v1.0.0, in two directions: **new silicon** —
eight more cards, a second video chip, OS-call tracing — and **a method
applied to the old silicon**: fetch the primary document rather than recall
it, and write down what is still not known.

- **No copyrighted firmware ships any more.** `roms/` tracks only the
  author's own and GPL material; a desktop package carries no dump and
  `stage_data.sh --verify` fails one that does. Without Apple's PROMs a
  Disk II boots on **POM2's own boot PROM and its own data separator**, which
  read and write WOZ. A first launch offers to download the rest, or drop a
  dump on the window.
- **Five cards and a VDP:** Microsoft SoftCard II, PCPI Applicard, Premium
  SoftCard //e, ALS "The CP/M Card", Saturn 128K, Videx UltraTerm, the DAN ][
  Controller, and the E-Z Color Graphics Interface with POM1's TMS9918A — the
  first card with a picture of its own, shown in a **Card Monitor** tab beside
  the Apple II screen.
- **OS-call tracing:** ProDOS MLI, SmartPort, Apple Pascal BIOS, the Monitor,
  Applecorn's BBC MOS, CP/M-65, CP/M BDOS on a card Z80, and the Disk II
  head's own line.
- **Disks from a URL, a `.zip` or a `.gz`**, cached locally so the guest's
  writes never touch the archive or the server.
- **Host file exchange:** one `NAME#TTAAAA` convention shared by the ProDOS
  host folder, `pom2_dos33` and `pom2_cpm`; CP/M text conversion; a guest LOCK
  that reaches the host file; and Integer BASIC listings.
- **Audio** survives a Raspberry Pi and a phone browser: the device buffer is
  a ladder climbed on late callbacks rather than a fixed guess.
- **Closer to the silicon, on the documents' authority.** The floating bus now
  reproduces every address in Sather's printed scanning maps, Figures 5.6 and
  5.9, including the HBL double-scan and the VBL wraparound. The Diablo 630's
  `ESC S`, `ESC X` and remote reset, the DS1216E's 12/24-hour mode, a //e
  RESET clearing the DHGR latch, the WOZ 2 Disk Sides field, the CRT
  scanline phase, a 400K WOZ mounting as a scrambled 800K volume — each fixed
  against a fetched primary source, and each pinned.
- **A fourth display aspect, `Measured`:** the shape a ruler finds on a
  correctly adjusted monitor (1.225:1 on NTSC, ~1.507 on PAL), which is
  neither 4:3 nor square pixels.
- **Many fixes** from six bug-hunt rounds against MAME, AppleWin, OpenEmulator
  and the data sheets — with what is *not* known recorded alongside, in
  `docs/audits/`.
- **An audit of every upstream citation, and a rule that came out of it.**
  Twenty-nine reports in `docs/audits/parite-*.md` date each of POM2's 801
  "MAME/AppleWin says" comments against the upstream file's full history and
  compare the behaviour, not the line number. The citations turned out to mix
  revisions from 2022 to 2026 with no pin; the comparison found **3 MAJEUR
  and 25 MINEUR**, all fixed against the primary document — a `SAVE` that
  disarmed the //c's motor timer, a 6551 interrupt the data sheet forbids
  (the "cable" that killed ProDOS was innocent), a Z8530 flow-control write
  that stopped the transmitter, a FLUX track losing one transition per
  revolution, five ImageWriter power-on defaults, two video timings measured
  on real machines, among others — and **28 places where MAME or AppleWin is
  wrong and POM2 is right**, now recorded so no future "alignment" undoes
  them. The rule: every claim about hardware carries its **evidence grade**
  in the code, `[A]` measured on the silicon down to `[F]` admitted unknown,
  and a citation of another emulator names its revision. The same table now
  heads every emulator of the family.
