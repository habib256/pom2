# POM2 v1.0.0

The first stable release: the Apple ][, ][+, //e, //c and //c Plus, in NTSC
and PAL, with the cards, media and tools listed in the README. What "1.0"
promises is written down in [the release plan](../backlog/release-1.0.md):
accurate claims, a repeatable build, and no known defect that silently loses
media, settings or snapshots.

- **Your data stays put:**
  - An unreadable `state.cfg` is kept aside instead of being replaced by
    defaults.
  - A disk on a drive that is not plugged in at launch keeps its place in the
    configuration.
  - Two POM2 windows no longer delete each other's printouts.
  - The browser never wipes its stored sessions when IndexedDB is slow to
    answer, and automatic resume falls back to the previous checkpoint.
- **Accelerators keep the crystal's time:** the beam, VBL, Disk II, SoftCard
  Z80, Liron IWM and paddles run at the real 1 MHz pace under a TransWarp,
  a Zip or the //c Plus.
- **Closer to the hardware:**
  - The composite colour phase is per scanline.
  - `.nib` sync follows MAME.
  - SSI263 phoneme lengths follow the data sheet.
  - The SoftCard Z80 stamps each bus access in its own cycle.
  - The 558 paddle timers cannot be retriggered while they count.
  - The ][+ cursor blinks from its own 555 timer.
- **Bundled disks:** A2 File Cmd 1.0, alongside Dirtbike 1.1,
  ChromaBreak 1.1, Micro-Sokoban 1.3 and DIX.
- **macOS:** a relative path on the command line works from inside the `.app`,
  and the first launch of a quarantined copy no longer quits.
- **Many fixes** from bug hunts against MAME, AppleWin and the
  data sheets: 6522, Z8530, 68705, TMS5220, printers, storage, rewind, the
  AI server and the keyboard.
