# POM2 v1.0.0

The first stable release: the Apple ][, ][+, //e, //c and //c Plus, in NTSC
and PAL, with the cards, media and tools listed in the README.

- **Your data stays put:** an unreadable `state.cfg`, a disk on an unplugged
  drive, the printouts of a second window and the browser's saved sessions
  are no longer lost.
- **Accelerators keep real time:** video, Disk II, SoftCard, Liron and
  paddles stay at 1 MHz under a TransWarp, a Zip or the //c Plus.
- **Closer to the hardware:** composite colour, `.nib` sync, SSI263 speech,
  paddle timers and the ][+ cursor.
- **Bundled disks:** A2 File Cmd 1.0, Dirtbike 1.1, ChromaBreak 1.1,
  Micro-Sokoban 1.3 and DIX.
- **macOS:** relative paths and the first launch of a downloaded copy work.
- **Many fixes** from bug hunts against MAME, AppleWin and the data sheets.
