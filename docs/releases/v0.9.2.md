# POM2 v0.9.2

**Six more rounds of adversarial hunters, twenty-four of them, on the parts
the first eleven rounds had never opened: the CPU's undocumented bus cycles,
the 3.5" codec, the speech chips, the cassette, the GL stage the screenshots
cannot see, and the tooling that builds the packages. Sixty-one defects, each
confirmed by a probe before it was touched and pinned by a test that fails
without the fix.**

33 commits since v0.9.1. Still no new hardware — but three of these reached
your files, one reached your terminal, and one shipped 55 MB of build
leftovers in every package you downloaded.

## Highlights

- **Write-protecting a mounted hard disk threw away your last writes.**
  Write-protect is the notch on the disk, and the panels flip it on a disk
  that is *in the drive* — but the flush was gated on the same test, so a
  disk protected after a guest write reported a successful save with the
  block still only in RAM, and the eject dropped it. The commit writes a
  temporary sibling and renames, which a read-only file accepts; the flush
  is gated on the image's own lock now and new writes stay refused.

- **A Disk II head that stopped at track 34.** The stepper clamp was ported
  with the *image's* 35 tracks where the drive's mechanical range is 42, so
  a seek past track 34 silently re-read track 34's sectors under the wrong
  number — on WOZ images whose quarter-track map really does fill 40 tracks.
  In the same pass, an erased stretch of surface read back as one constant
  byte, identical on every revolution: the opposite of the coin toss a
  weak-bit copy protection measures. It is MAME's 4 µs pulse train now.

- **Every SSI263 phoneme played the sound two codes higher.** The phoneme
  table is AppleWin's byte for byte, and AppleWin indexes it by code minus
  two with code zero as the pause. Speech on the Echo+ and the Mockingboard
  Sound II said the wrong thing, audibly paused where it should have been
  silent, and went mute on the last two codes.

- **The crackle was the printer.** The grain scheduler clamped its cursor
  onto its lead cap while the drop gate was strictly greater, so once a
  burst reached the cap every later grain landed on the *same* audio frame
  and their attacks summed — one carriage return parks the cursor, and the
  line that follows arrives in a single tick. 0.44 full-scale per printed
  line, four times the detector's own bar. The mixer also grew its own
  clicks/s row: the per-source tally is pre-sum and pre-gain, so clipping
  and every mixer-side cut had no row to point at.

- **Double lo-res was one dot to the right.** The colour rotation that
  belongs to the demodulator was baked into the bit stream and the
  80-column phase term was left at zero; the two cancelled in colour and
  showed in monochrome and in the composite signal — half the samples of a
  frame wrong against MAME's own word builder. Eight goldens re-recorded
  with the reason written down.

- **The CRT glass pass aliased every 560-wide picture in a narrow panel.**
  DHGR, 80 columns, the Le Chat Mauve and the composite demod are 560 dots
  wide while the screen target is sized from the 280-dot geometry, so a
  docked or short window point-sampled them 2:1 with no filter at all:
  scattered white dots, dotted lines, fine detail dropping out. And every
  window resize re-lit the black border outside the barrel at 40 % white.

- **A filename could forge a log line.** The logger printed its message
  verbatim and the message routinely carries a path out of `state.cfg` or
  a name off your disk — any byte but `/` and NUL. A disk named `a.dsk`
  followed by a newline and `[ERROR] ROM: …` produced a report POM2 never
  wrote; an escape sequence cleared your screen or renamed the window.

- **The guest could still reach your loopback.** libslirp's own
  `disable_host_loopback` refuses the `10.0.2.2` alias route and nothing
  else, so a guest frame naming `127.0.0.1` directly was dialled with the
  shipped defaults — through the CS8900A, the W5100's MACRAW, and its
  IP-raw path, which never checked its destination at all.

- **Every package shipped the developer SDK.** A CMake `COMPONENT` names a
  subset for `--install --component`; it does not keep that subset out of a
  plain `cmake --install`, which is what every packager runs. Each
  AppImage, `.deb` and tarball carried `libpom2_core.a`, the headers and
  the CMake package — 55 MB — and nothing looked, because `--verify` is
  pointed at the data directory.

- **Sixteen tests had quietly left the harness.** The directory-wide ctest
  sweeps — skip semantics, the timeout floor, and the protected-media
  environment that keeps the suite off your disk images — sat *above* every
  test appended since 2026-09-07. Those ran with media writable. The sweeps
  are last in the file now, and a test reads the generated list back so the
  next append cannot repeat it.

## Also in this release

- **The 6502.** The undocumented read opcodes never touched the bus: on the
  Unenhanced //e — the machine A2 File Cmd's 6502 build and the French
  Touch corpus run on — `NOP $C030` did not click the speaker and
  `LAX $C0EC` did not advance the Disk II latch. All 256 opcodes of both
  cores were then swept for cycles and lengths, the `$C0xx` bus traces,
  the interrupt and reset sequences, decimal mode, the Z80's interrupt
  layer and the SoftCard's DMA: clean.

- **The Disk II write path.** A burst that continued after a mid-session
  flush was spliced twice and lost nibbles; a stepper pulse moved the head
  without committing, so a write to one track landed on its neighbour; a
  nibble write on a WOZ blanked the quarter-track for the session.

- **The SmartPort.** A snapshot written before the unit count grew ate four
  bytes of its owner's state and served a write to a device the host never
  named; `$C0n0` unit-select reached a bay past the count the card answers
  for; a call reported the *previous* ProDOS transfer's error, so one
  failing volume scan poisoned the session; a 3.5" flux write committed
  sectors to the far side of the platter without checking the head.

- **The timers.** The accelerator's device-clock fan-out ran a frame behind
  the chunk loop, so every emuCycles consumer spent a transition frame 2.6×
  out; the Phasor's AY clock scale was applied 40 ms early and its /RESET
  strobe flooded the event queue; the ThunderClock+'s TP output ran 0.12 %
  slow at 2048 Hz because its half-period was rounded to a whole cycle.

- **The telnet console** could type but never interrupt, correct or escape:
  the keyboard bridge fed the *clipboard* paste, whose filter drops every
  control byte. ENTER arrived twice under negotiated BINARY, a new client
  inherited the previous one's unread typing, and the ACIA's data register
  answered a second read with a NUL.

- **The paging latches.** The `$C800` expansion-window owner was in no
  snapshot, so a restore whose PC sat inside a card's ROM fetched the
  floating bus; the debugger's write-target peek returned ROM above `$C000`,
  so undoing a Memory Viewer edit wrote a ROM byte into Language-Card RAM.

- **The //c.** "Internal 3.5" selected" lived in two places and the ROM bank
  switch cleared only one, so a //c+ rewind lost its internal drive; two
  status reads answered the floating bus where MAME's //c answers the latch.
  A `$C060` read — the 40/80-column switch there — started the cassette.

- **The cassette** round-trips through the real Monitor ROM, WRITE on one
  machine and READ on a fresh one, `.aci` and `.wav`, four sample rates,
  NTSC and PAL. Extensible-format WAVs (what ffmpeg and Audacity emit) were
  refused; a `data` chunk longer than the file killed the tape; a line-in
  rip on a DC bias decoded as silence.

- **The Workstation Card's** 65C02 ran 22 % fast on a running machine
  because it discarded the cycles it overshot per call — and the slot bus
  calls it once per host instruction. Its Z8530 lost the tail of every
  received frame with neither the overrun bit nor End-Of-Frame set.

- **The printer's paper tray.** "Clear all" power-cycled the printer after
  emptying the tray, and the power cycle ejects the platen's sheet — back
  into the tray, and into the durable print history. A form feed in the
  data stream went through the front-panel button's blank-sheet rule on the
  Epson and Diablo heads, dropping pages.

- **The paint editor's fill** stopped one column short of the canvas edge —
  192 bytes of the old picture left behind on a solid recolour — and the
  clipboard took its block geometry from the current mode rather than from
  the clip.

- **Storage, before the hunts.** Write-protect became the notch on the
  disk, modelled as the image file's read-only bit and flipped from any
  media panel or the Disk Library's right-click menu; the SmartPort card
  and the //c rear-port chain take eight units (eleven ProDOS devices on a
  //c); the Disk Library grew a NeoST-style mounted block with per-volume
  eject and notch buttons, and an HDV mount adds a volume into the first
  free bay instead of replacing the last one.

- **Legal and release.** `THIRD-PARTY.md` and the font licences ship; the
  browser demo boots DIX; every workflow action is pinned to a commit and
  the release is rehearsed weekly; the About box names the author.

## What was cleared, and how

Nine subsystems were checked term-for-term against their oracles and found
correct — that is the other half of a hunt, and it is why this list is
shorter than the last one relative to the work: the display painters (HGR,
DHGR, lo-res, all 256 text codes, the Video-7 and Chat Mauve modes, the
AppleWin NTSC tables) dot for dot against MAME and AppleWin; the 3.5" GCR
codec across every sector of an 800K image; the AY-3-8910's envelopes,
noise LFSR, mixer and read masks; the 6522 under batched advances; the
W5100's ring wrap in both conventions; the ProDOS block protocol and the
host folder's volume structures; the settings parser over 422 hostile
cases; fifty CLI invocations; and the CAM16 colour model against the
paper's reference values.

## Install

Same as v0.9.1 — download the package for your machine from the release
page, or [play it in the browser](https://habib256.github.io/pom2/wasm/).
Your `state.cfg` carries over untouched.
