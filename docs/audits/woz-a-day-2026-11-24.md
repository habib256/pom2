# woz-a-day through POM2, MAME and AppleWin — 2026-11-24

4am's woz-a-day collection (2833 WOZ boot disks, fetched from archive.org by
`tools/woz_corpus_fetch.py`; the disks are not in the repository) booted in
three emulators by `tools/woz_corpus.py`. 588 are out of scope (3.5-inch or
IIgs only); **2245 titles** are compared.

## Method

- **Same machine for all three**: the one the WOZ INFO chunk asks for —
  enhanced //e when it lists it or says nothing, else unenhanced //e, ][+,
  ][; a 13-sector disk that names the original ][ runs on it (its programs
  are mostly Integer BASIC). An older WOZ with no INFO hardware word is read
  from META's `requires_machine`. No card beside the Disk II.
- **Same emulated time**: 45 s. A black picture or the bare banner gets a
  look at 50 s and at 100 s in every emulator (a title can be between two
  screens at one instant; some load for over 45 s at the drive's speed).
- **Same witness**, from the 40-column text page and the picture: `error`
  (a boot or I/O message, `LANGUAGE NOT AVAILABLE` included), `crash` (the
  Monitor's register line), `noboot` (only the power-on banner), `blank`,
  else `screen` — the pass. A message counts only when the picture is a text
  screen (a game in graphics can leave a stale one on the text page).
  Coarse, and the same for all three: what it measures is agreement.
- **Versions**: POM2 libretro core at the v1.2.3 tag with original drive timing;
  AppleWin's libretro core (audetto/AppleWin 6e6df2cb); MAME 0.288,
  drivers apple2ee / apple2e / apple2p / apple2, firmware from RetroBIOS.
  MAME's `diskii13` set lacks the 341-0009 dump: for 13-sector disks it was
  given Apple's (`-sl6 diskiing13`), and the original ][, which has no
  Autostart, is started with `C600G`, as on the real machine.
- POM2 ran with **no Apple Disk II firmware**: its own 16- and 13-sector boot
  PROMs and its own sequencer.

## What the run fixed in POM2 (2026-11-23 → 24)

From 97.1 % (tied with MAME) to 99.3 %, and no title any other emulator
boots that POM2 does not:

- an empty drive's data register stood still (4am's Anti-M asked forever);
- weak bits drawn independently (SSI's RDOS check: Battalion Commander,
  Bomb Alley, Carrier Force), and placed as the WOZ reference says — from
  the 3rd zero of a 4-zero run (Decimal and Percent Word Problems);
- the platter's speed drift made continuous in time: 13 titles;
- POM2's own 13-sector boot PROM: 19 DOS 3.2 titles that no emulator
  booted without Apple's 341-0009;
- an older WOZ's machine read from META (Genetic Drift, Pest Patrol on the
  ][+ they ask for).

## Open

What POM2 does not boot, no emulator boots. Its own META explains most of
it (see the list above): the Nibbles Away II auto-load files and the
Wizimore scenarios are data disks for another program, and Animal Facts
needs a separate boot disk. The Snapper runs on a ][, ][+ or //e and stays
black on a //e Enhanced in all three emulators: a 65C02 incompatibility,
very likely the iron's too.

Reproduce: `tools/woz_corpus_fetch.py`, then the command at the top of
`tools/woz_corpus.py`.
