Editable SVG sources are included alongside the PNG textures.
POM2 adapts the Monitor II bezel to its hard-disk beige palette and removes
the top and bottom decorative emblems.
The Apple 3.5 front illustration is drawn for POM2 from a user-supplied
reference photograph. It reuses the MIT rainbow emblem geometry from the
Disk II SVG; the photograph itself is not distributed.
The Disk //c front illustration is drawn for POM2 from a user-supplied
reference photograph, with its central latch and light grey case.
The photograph itself is not distributed.
The Apple hard-disk front illustration is drawn for POM2 from a user-supplied
reference photograph and reuses the MIT rainbow emblem geometry.
The photograph itself is not distributed.
No drive recordings or firmware are included.
